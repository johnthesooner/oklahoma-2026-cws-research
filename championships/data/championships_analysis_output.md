## The shape of the dynasty

- **27 of 46** OU national titles (59%) have come in **2000 or later**.
- **37 of 46** (80%) are in **Olympic / non-revenue sports** (gymnastics, softball, wrestling, golf) — not football or baseball.
- Football is the brand, but its **7** titles are **15%** of the total and the last came in **2000**.
- **Men's gymnastics (12)** is OU's single most-decorated program — more national titles than any other OU sport.
