By coaching era

The two postseason columns differ only by conference championship games, where Stoops went 7-1 and Riley 4-0. An earlier version of this project published only the combined figure and so showed both coaches with winning postseason records; their bowl-and-playoff records are 9-9 and 2-3.

Seasons

Percentiles are within the 27 seasons shown here, not nationally. Luck is actual wins minus Pythagorean expectation at exponent 2.37; the Stoops-era total is deliberately not reported anywhere in this project because it is not robust to the exponent.

Games

Site is the physical location, not the designated home team: the 2021 Tulane game is coded home because a hurricane relocated it to Norman. Opponent rank is the AP rank at kickoff; two were corrected against Oklahoma's own season articles and are noted in the audit trail.
